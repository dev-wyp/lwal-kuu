# Lwal Kuu — Customer Onboarding Guide

Welcome to **Lwal Kuu**! This guide assists shop owners, wholesale distributors, and staff members in getting started with Lwal Kuu in under 5 minutes.

---

## 1. Quick Start in 4 Easy Steps

### Step 1: Register Your Account
1. Visit the [Registration Page](/register).
2. Enter your Full Name, Phone Number, Email, and Password.
3. Click **Continue to Organization Setup**.

### Step 2: Set Up Your Shop Organization
1. Provide your **Shop Name** (e.g. *Shwe Thiri Wholesale Store*).
2. A unique URL slug is generated automatically.
3. Your default currency is configured to **Myanmar Kyat (MMK)** and timezone to **Asia/Yangon**.
4. Click **Launch Lwal Kuu**. Your **14-day free trial** is immediately activated!

### Step 3: Add Your Inventory & Products
1. Navigate to **Products** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add Product** and enter:
   - SKU Code (e.g., `OIL-001`)
   - Product Name (e.g., *Palm Oil 18L Tin*)
   - Unit of Measurement (e.g., *Box / Pcs*)
   - Cost Price & Selling Price
   - Low Stock Alert Threshold (e.g., *10 units*)
3. Save to immediately populate your catalog.

### Step 4: Record Initial Stock & Suppliers
- To record incoming shipments, navigate to **Purchases** &rarr; **New Purchase Order**.
- When goods arrive at your shop, click **Receive Goods**. The stock is automatically added to your inventory ledger, and the weighted average cost is updated!

---

## 2. Managing Daily Sales & Customer Credit

### Making a Sale:
1. Open the **POS / Sales** register from the top navbar or sidebar.
2. Click or tap any available product to add it to your cart.
3. Select an existing customer or leave as *Walk-in Customer*.
4. Choose Payment Terms:
   - **Full Cash:** Customer pays the full balance upfront.
   - **Partial Paid:** Customer deposits part of the total (e.g. 50,000 Ks on a 200,000 Ks invoice).
   - **Full Credit:** Zero upfront payment. Balance is automatically tracked in the customer's ledger.
5. Click **Confirm Sale & Print** to print the thermal or standard receipt.

### Collecting Credit Payments:
1. Navigate to **Customer Payments** &rarr; **Receive Payment**.
2. Select the customer remitting funds.
3. Click **Auto-Allocate (FIFO)** to automatically distribute the payment across their oldest unpaid invoices, or manually allocate specific amounts.
4. The customer's statement is instantly updated with zero drift.

## 3. Team Member Roles

- **Owner:** Full access, subscription billing, organization settings.
- **Admin:** Manage team members, products, pricing, and void transactions.
- **Manager:** Create purchases, manage inventory adjustments, view financial reports.
- **Staff:** Fast checkout, sales invoicing, customer directory, payment collection.
